# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give constructive feedback and explain technical disagreements calmly.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy. Never publish real PANs, personal records, credentials, or private communications.
- Help keep discussions relevant, factual, and useful.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private or sensitive information.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith participation.
- Sharing credentials, PANs, or private registrar responses without authorization.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository owner through the contact options on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/SatyamGangani). Do not post private or sensitive evidence in a public issue.

Maintainers are expected to review reports fairly and take action they consider appropriate to protect the community.
